Who We Are

An Alliance Built on Conscience

GELA is not a political movement or a software project. It is a living alliance of thinkers, youth leaders, educators, and activists who believe that every system — from AI to governance — must be designed with ethical intention.

Born in Dhaka in February 2026, GELA draws on the values of Islamic ethics (Adl, Amanah, Tawhid, Ilm), universal human rights, and the UN Sustainable Development Goals — weaving them into a single, coherent framework: Conscious Systems Design.

Our Core Belief: Technology must serve humanity — not control it. Leadership must be ethical — not power-hungry. Justice is not a policy position. It is a moral obligation — a form of worship.

The Ethical Kernel

Six Axioms of Ethical Leadership — the moral constitution of GELA.

01 Tawhid — Unity of Purpose All action flows from a single, integrated moral source. No contradiction between faith, reason, and justice.
02 Adl — Justice as Foundation Justice is not a policy option — it is the structural baseline of every legitimate system.
03 Amanah — Sacred Trust Leadership is a trust (amanah), not a privilege. Power held in stewardship.
04 Ilm — Knowledge with Conscience Knowledge pursued without ethical grounding is dangerous. Learning must serve human flourishing.
05 Rahmah — Universal Compassion Systems protect the most vulnerable. Mercy is a structural requirement.
06 Shura — Collective Deliberation Decisions affecting communities must involve those communities. Inclusion is not optional.
