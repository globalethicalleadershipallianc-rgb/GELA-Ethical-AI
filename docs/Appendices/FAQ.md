# GELA FAQ v1.0

---

## General Questions

### What is GELA?

GELA (Global Ethical Leadership Alliance) is an open-source, decentralized movement for ethical leadership and war prevention.

### Is GELA a political organization?

No. GELA is apolitical. We welcome members from all countries and political systems.

### How is GELA funded?

Through membership fees, donations, and grants. All finances are transparent and recorded on-chain.

---

## Membership Questions

### How do I become a member?

Apply online, pay the membership fee, and sign the charter. You will receive a membership NFT.

### What are the membership types?

- **Sovereign** (countries): $10,000/year – Council seat, full voting
- **Associate** (NGOs/universities): $1,000/year – Observer status
- **Individual**: $50/year – PVT token, voting rights
- **Honorary**: $0 – Founder's nomination

### Can I join for free?

Yes, if you are nominated as an honorary member.

---

## Technical Questions

### What is HUMANE?

HUMANE is GELA's war prevention system with 6 layers: sensors, ECE, hardlock, mediation, PVT, and monitoring.

### How does the People's Veto work?

Any member can propose a veto against a war. If 51% of votes cast are vetoes, the war is stopped.

### Is my vote private?

Yes. We use zero-knowledge proofs (ZK-proofs) for voter anonymity.

---

## Governance Questions

### Who controls GELA?

No single entity. GELA is governed by its members through the Council and the People's Veto.

### How are decisions made?

- War decisions: People's veto (51% threshold)
- Strategic decisions: Council vote (2/3 majority)
- Budget: Council vote (simple majority)

---

## Emergency Questions

### What happens if a war is already happening?

GELA's sensors detect it early. The People's Veto can still stop escalation.

### How fast is the response?

Sensors detect in real-time (< 1 second). Veto voting takes 1-7 days. Auto mediation is instant.
