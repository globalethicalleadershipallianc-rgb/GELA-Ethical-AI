# GELA Glossary v1.0

---

## A

**AGES** – Alliance for Global Ethical Stewardship. GELA's training and certification program for ethical leaders.

**Amanah** – Trustworthiness. One of GELA's six ethical axioms. Leadership as sacred trust.

**Adl** – Justice. One of GELA's six ethical axioms. Justice as the foundation of every system.

---

## C

**Charter** – GELA's founding document, defining governance, membership, and decision-making.

**CSD** – Conscious Systems Design. GELA's framework for building just, resilient, and humane systems.

---

## E

**ECE** – Ethical Consequence Engine. AI system that simulates war consequences.

**Ethical Kernel** – Six axioms (Tawhid, Adl, Amanah, Ilm, Rahmah, Shura) forming GELA's moral foundation.

---

## G

**GELA** – Global Ethical Leadership Alliance. An open-source, decentralized movement for ethical leadership and war prevention.

---

## H

**HUMANE** – Holistic Universal Mechanism for Absolute Nonviolence & Ethics. GELA's war prevention system.

---

## I

**Ilm** – Knowledge. One of GELA's six ethical axioms. Knowledge with conscience.

---

## M

**Membership NFT** – Non-fungible token proving GELA membership.

---

## P

**PVT** – People's Veto Token. Blockchain-based voting token for war prevention.

---

## R

**Rahmah** – Compassion. One of GELA's six ethical axioms. Universal compassion.

---

## S

**Shura** – Collective deliberation. One of GELA's six ethical axioms. Collaborative governance.

---

## T

**Tawhid** – Unity of purpose. One of GELA's six ethical axioms.
