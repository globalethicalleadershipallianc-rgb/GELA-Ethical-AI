# 🌍 GELA Vision Statement v1.0

**Global Ethical Leadership Alliance**

---

## Our Vision

A world where war is not possible — because ethics, technology, and the people's veto together close every path to war before it begins.

---

## Our Mission

- Build a global platform for ethical leadership
- Deploy technology to prevent war
- Give people direct decision-making power
- Establish transparent and accountable governance structures

---

## Core Values

| Value | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Peace First** | War is the last resort, never the first option |
| **People's Power** | The people have the final say on war and peace |
| **Radical Transparency** | Every decision, every vote, every outcome is public |
| **Ethical Technology** | AI and blockchain serve humanity, not control it |
| **Global Solidarity** | No nation or group stands alone |

---

## Our Promise

GELA is not controlled by any country, party, or individual. It is a decentralized, self-governing alliance where every member has an equal voice.

---

## Three Pillars

⚖️ **Justice is Worship**  
🤝 **Unity is Strength**  
🌍 **Humanity is Destiny**
