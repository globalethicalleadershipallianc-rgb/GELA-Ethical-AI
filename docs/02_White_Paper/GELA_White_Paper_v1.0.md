# GELA White Paper v1.0

**Global Ethical Leadership Alliance**

---

## Executive Summary

GELA is a decentralized, open-source movement for ethical leadership and war prevention. It combines:

- A multi-agent AI system for ethical decision-making
- A blockchain-based voting mechanism (People's Veto Token)
- A global sensor network for conflict detection
- A training academy for ethical leadership

## Governance Model

### GELA Council Structure

- **Sovereign Members (countries)** – Council seat, full voting
- **Associate Members (NGOs, universities)** – Observer status
- **Individual Members** – PVT token, voting rights
- **Honorary Members** – Founder's nomination

### Voting Mechanisms

- **Public Referendum:** Required for war decisions
- **People's Veto:** 51% threshold stops any war proposal
- **Council Vote:** Strategic and budgetary decisions
