# GELA Membership Guidelines v1.0

---

## 1. Membership Types at a Glance

| Type | Fee (Annual) | Benefits |
|------|--------------|----------|
| **Sovereign (Country)** | $10,000 | Council seat, full voting, all projects |
| **Associate (NGO/University)** | $1,000 | Observer status, training access |
| **Individual** | $50 | PVT token, courses, voting |
| **Honorary** | $0 | All benefits (Founder's nomination) |

---

## 2. How to Apply

**Step-by-step process:**

1. Fill online application form (link on GELA website)
2. Submit required documents:
   - For Sovereign: Letter from government
   - For Associate: NGO registration certificate
   - For Individual: ID card / passport copy
3. Pay membership fee (crypto or bank transfer)
4. Sign charter (via smart contract or wet signature)
5. Receive membership NFT (proof of membership)

**Processing time:** 3-5 business days

---

## 3. Member Responsibilities

All members agree to:

- Abide by GELA Charter
- Pay fees on time
- Participate in votes (at least 30% of proposals)
- Promote peace in their communities
- Report violations to the Ethics Committee
