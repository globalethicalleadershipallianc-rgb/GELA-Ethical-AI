# GELA Charter v1.0

**Global Ethical Leadership Alliance**

---

## Preamble

We, the undersigned, establish this Global Ethical Leadership Alliance (GELA) to:

- Prevent war through ethical technology and collective action
- Uphold justice, unity, and humanity as our guiding principles
- Empower people with direct decision-making power over war and peace
- Build a transparent, accountable, and decentralized global governance framework

---

## Chapter 1: Fundamental Principles

### Article 1: Definition

GELA is a decentralized, open-source, non-profit alliance of individuals, organizations, and nations committed to ethical leadership and war prevention.

### Article 2: Core Principles (Five Pillars)

1. **Peace First** – War is never the first option
2. **People's Power** – The people have the final veto
3. **Radical Transparency** – All decisions are public
4. **Ethical Technology** – AI and blockchain serve humanity
5. **Global Solidarity** – No one stands alone

### Article 3: Objectives

- Prevent armed conflict before it starts
- Provide real-time war detection and alerts
- Enable people's veto on war proposals
- Train ethical leaders globally
- Build a just, cooperative world order

---

## Chapter 2: Membership

### Article 4: Types of Membership

| Type | Eligibility | Fee | Benefits |
|------|-------------|-----|----------|
| **Sovereign** | UN member state | $10,000/year | Council seat, full voting, all projects |
| **Associate** | NGO, university | $1,000/year | Observer status, training access |
| **Individual** | Any person | $50/year | PVT token, courses, voting |
| **Honorary** | Founder's nomination | $0 | All benefits |

### Article 5: Admission Process

1. Submit application
2. Pay membership fee (crypto or fiat)
3. Sign the charter (smart contract)
4. Receive membership NFT

### Article 6: Rights and Duties

**Rights:**
- Participate in votes (according to membership type)
- Access conflict alerts
- Take AGES training courses
- Propose new projects

**Duties:**
- Abide by the charter
- Pay fees on time
- Promote peace
- Report violations

### Article 7: Suspension and Expulsion

A member may be suspended or expelled for:
- Violation of charter principles
- Incitement to violence
- Misuse of GELA resources
- Non-payment of fees

---

## Chapter 3: Governance

### Article 8: GELA Council

The Council consists of:
- All Sovereign members
- 5 elected Associate representatives
- 10 elected Individual representatives
- Founder (permanent seat)

### Article 9: Decision Making

| Decision Type | Mechanism |
|---------------|-----------|
| War prevention | People's veto (51% threshold) |
| Strategic | Council vote (2/3 majority) |
| Budgetary | Council vote (simple majority) |
| Charter amendment | 3/4 majority + Founder approval |

### Article 10: Veto System

- Any member can propose a veto against a war
- 51% of total votes cast stops the war
- Votes are cast via PVT token on Polygon blockchain

## Chapter 6: Ethics and Discipline

### Article 17: Code of Conduct

All members must:
- Uphold justice, unity, and humanity
- Reject violence and incitement
- Respect diversity of opinion
- Maintain transparency

### Article 18: Ethics Committee

The Ethics Committee consists of 7 members elected by the Council. It investigates violations and recommends sanctions.

### Article 19: Penalties

| Violation Level | Penalty |
|----------------|---------|
| Minor | Warning |
| Moderate | Suspension (up to 1 year) |
| Severe | Expulsion |

---

## Chapter 7: Amendment

### Article 20: Amendment Process

1. Proposal by any Council member
2. 30-day public discussion
3. Council vote (2/3 majority)
4. Founder approval (if charter core is affected)
5. Smart contract update
