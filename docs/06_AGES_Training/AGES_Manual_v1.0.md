# AGES Training Manual v1.0

**Alliance for Global Ethical Stewardship**

---

## Course Overview

AGES is GELA's ethical leadership training program. Four courses, each 2-3 weeks.

---

## Course 1: Introduction to Ethical Leadership

**Duration:** 2 weeks  
**Certificate:** GELA Ethical Leader (Level 1)

### Modules

| Module | Topics |
|--------|--------|
| 1 | What is ethical leadership? |
| 2 | Case studies from history (Gandhi, MLK, Mandela) |
| 3 | The five pillars of ethical leadership |
| 4 | Self-assessment and leadership style |

### Learning Objectives

- Define ethical leadership
- Identify ethical dilemmas
- Apply ethical frameworks to real situations
- Reflect on personal leadership values

---

## Course 2: Blockchain for Governance

**Duration:** 3 weeks  
**Certificate:** GELA Blockchain Governance (Level 2)

### Modules

| Module | Topics |
|--------|--------|
| 1 | Basics of blockchain and cryptography |
| 2 | Smart contracts and DAOs |
| 3 | Voting mechanisms (token-based, quadratic, veto) |
| 4 | GELA's PVT token in practice |

### Learning Objectives

- Explain blockchain basics
- Understand smart contract functions
- Participate in a mock DAO vote
- Analyze GELA's veto mechanism

---

## Course 3: Conflict Resolution

---

## Certification

### Level 1: GELA Ethical Leader

- Complete Course 1
- Pass quiz (80%+)
- Sign charter

### Level 2: GELA Advanced Certified

- Complete all 4 courses
- Pass all quizzes
- Complete a capstone project
- Be an active member for 6+ months

### Level 3: GELA Trainer

- Level 2 certified
- Train 20+ new members
- Appointed by Council
